# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Name
Nova Retail – Sales & Customer Analytics

## Business Scenario

Nova Retail is a fictional multi-vendor e-commerce marketplace operating across Brazil. The company wants to improve its business performance by analyzing sales, customer behavior, product performance, seller performance, payment patterns, customer satisfaction, and delivery efficiency.

The management team has requested the Data Analytics team to build a centralized analytics solution that transforms raw operational data into meaningful business insights. The solution will support data-driven decision-making through SQL analysis and interactive Power BI dashboards.

---

# Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall business performance.
- Identify revenue and sales trends.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Evaluate product and category performance.
- Measure seller performance across regions.
- Analyze payment preferences and installment behavior.
- Measure customer satisfaction using review scores.
- Evaluate delivery performance and shipping efficiency.
- Build interactive dashboards for business stakeholders.
- Provide actionable recommendations based on analytical findings.

# Key Business Questions

## Sales Analysis

- What is the total revenue generated?
- How many orders have been placed?
- What is the average order value?
- How do monthly sales change over time?
- Which states generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
- Which months perform best?

---

## Customer Analysis

- How many unique customers exist?
- What percentage of customers are repeat customers?
- Which cities have the highest customer concentration?
- Which states generate the highest number of orders?
- Who are the highest-value customers?
- What is the average customer lifetime value?

---

## Product Analysis

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which product categories perform best?
- Which products sell the least?
- Which categories have the highest average selling price?
- Which products have the highest freight cost?

---

## Seller Analysis

- How many active sellers exist?
- Which sellers generate the highest revenue?
- Which states have the highest-performing sellers?
- Which sellers process the largest number of orders?

---

## Payment Analysis

- Which payment method is most frequently used?
- How many customers choose installment payments?
- What is the average payment amount?
- How many payment methods are used across all transactions?

---

## Customer Satisfaction

- What is the average review score?
- Which product categories receive the highest ratings?
- Which categories receive poor ratings?
- Is there a relationship between delivery delays and review scores?

---

## Delivery Analysis

- What is the average delivery time?
- Which states experience the longest delivery times?
- How many orders were delivered late?
- Which sellers consistently deliver on time?

The project will be considered successful if it:

- Organizes raw data into a well-structured relational database.
- Produces accurate and validated analytical insights.
- Supports business decision-making through dashboards.
- Demonstrates end-to-end data analytics skills suitable for a professional portfolio.
